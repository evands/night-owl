Preset

A preset is a saved look: colours, effect, brightness and pulse shape. Choosing one changes the lights at once. Light lead and the deck options stay as they are, because they belong to this setup, not to a look.

Loading the Entertainment areas…
Normally the followers take turns one at a time. This sends twice as many commands, which is about all the bridge accepts while it is also streaming. If the followers start to lag, or the streamed lights stutter, turn it off.
A Hue bridge streams to one Entertainment area at a time, and an area holds at most ten lights. For a bigger room, make more areas in the Hue app, place their lights in the same room layout, and tick them all here. The area with the most channels is streamed on every beat. Lights that are only in the other areas follow by ordinary commands, which the bridge accepts far more slowly: they take the show\u2019s colours and take turns flashing, one light on each beat, the turn moving across the room. Put the lights that matter most in the biggest area.
Changes apply instantly and are saved.
